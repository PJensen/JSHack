# Preserve Daylight Mystery In Overworld Fog

## Summary
Daytime overworld fog fades because ambient sunlight is fed into the lighting engine as full-scene light. At noon, `computeAmbient()` returns roughly `[0.82, 0.81, 0.78]`; `engine.js` sums that and applies `brightness = min(1, lightSum * 0.5)`, so daylight alone saturates `lightLift` to `1`. Since explored tiles are drawn at full alpha when lighting is active, the darkness overlay becomes nearly transparent everywhere sunlight reaches.
