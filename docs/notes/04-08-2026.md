## Playtesting Notes

# broadly speaking

- the game plays well overall -- good!
- when equipping an item, the expected micro-ux is not working as anticipated, in a shop however, when selling items it did work as expected -- I expected the drop and equip etc, to show the same pulses. [fix-me]
- holding a (movement) keyboard key down while "fast" becomes ungoverned visually very quickly. this I think is acceptable for now. [wont-fix]
- there was so much loot, the the enemies so easy that I felt very little need to equip anything but for an epic because it was clearly better. I would not be against an 'auto-loadout` feature that auto-equips the best of carried equipment.
- potions felt inaccessible despite having quick items -- we definately need keybindings for items, and, on mobile, we need either a quick double tap to use. I legitimately died while having health potions
- I want players to lean on the enviornment; to lean on their items; to lean on their gathered arcane knowledge of the game and its systems to "survive" (and maybe thrive)
- as regards loot, part of whats happening is that a monster will drop all of their equipped gear; I'm broadly fine with this for story telling -- you know, a goblin dropping goblin gear, but it's bothering me the amount of gear that is being dropped on every single goblin.
- arrows should almost certainly be auto-pickup, they weigh nothing, they're inexpensive to carry, certainly if the player has a rangeed weapon equipped this is virtually a must do. maybe we're giving away too many arrows for free.

## Meta

- at the meta level, I feel a strong push-and-pull between an arcade style game and a very tactical, every turn counts feeling when I put my game designer hat on -- I feel that we could go either way; however, I worry that the true depth of the game is overlooked when you are flying through.

### Granted

- really talented nethack players move at astonishing speed -- I don't have a clear anwwer

## Potions

- health potions too abundant, too strong
- mana potions, barely used, barely needed
- generators -- are far too strong

## Balance

- I'm burning through everything -- I want my hits to mean something, I want TTK to feel viseral, I want encounters to be more dynamic, entangle needs to really be strategic, 3 monsters in a room should lead to "ohhhh shit, I need to use my tools" -- not, " I can burn these down easily"

## Overall

- lots of fine tuning to do
- lots of balance to do
